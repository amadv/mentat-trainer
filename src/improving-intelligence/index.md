# Improving Intelligence

What possible ways are there of improving someone's general intelligence?

Activities which have been suggested, with varying amounts of supporting evidence, include:

* [PhysicalExercise](/improving-intelligence/physical-exercise)
* [BrainTrainingGames](/improving-intelligence/brain-training-games)
* [MediTation](/improving-intelligence/meditation) and other techniques to [LowerStress](http://www.sfn.org/index.cfm?pagename=brainBriefings_stressAndTheBrain)
* [LanguageLearning](/other-pages/language-learning)
* [BioFeedback](/improving-intelligence/bio-feedback)
* [SmartFood](/improving-intelligence/smart-food)
* [SmartDrug](/improving-intelligence/smart-drug)
* [GrowthMindset](/other-pages/growth-mindset)
* [ArtsTraining](http://www.dana.org/uploadedFiles/News_and_Publications/Special_Publications/Learning,%20Arts%20and%20the%20Brain_ArtsAndCognition_Compl.pdf) - perhaps training in music, dance and drama can improve cognitive performance?
* [SocialInteraction](http://www.eurekalert.org/pub_releases/2007-10/uom-tmo102907.php)
