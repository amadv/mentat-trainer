# Bio Feedback

Can we use biofeedback techniques to improve mental functioning?

Some initial background links:

* [Wikipedia article on Biofeedback](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biofeedback)
* ["Efficacy levels" of different Biofeedback treatements from AAPB](http://www.aapb.org/consumer_efficacy.html)

  Biofeedback and training of attention
  -------------------------------------

  Biofeedback may be an effective treatment for attention deficit disorders; it might therefore be a way of improving focus in people without deficits. See:
* [ADD and ADHD](http://www.aapb.org/i4a/pages/index.cfm?pageid=3330) at applied psychophysiology and biofeedback
* [Abstracts from the Journal of Neurotherapy](http://www.isnr.org/uploads/Volume%204.pdf), including a number of relevant papers
* [How strong is the research support for neurofeedback treatment of children with adhd?](http://www.sharpbrains.com/blog/2008/01/25/how-strong-is-the-research-support-for-neurofeedback-treatment-of-children-with-adhd/) - blog post
