# The Name Mentat

:   *What does "Mentat" mean?*

The word *mentat* comes from [Frank Herbert](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Herbert)'s [Dune novels,](http://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Dune_(novel)&oldid=296647729) and refers to a person who undergoes special training to become a "human computer" in a society where computers are taboo. Although computers are not a taboo subject on this wiki (you're soaking in them!), augmentation of the human brain by computer is not generally a topic of interest; that information should be delegated to [a Transhumanist wiki,](http://sl4.org/bin/wiki.pl?HomePage) except where the computer is used for training (example: drilling on the pegs in a [PegSystem](/memory-technique/peg-system)).

Wikipedia has a [good article on mentats in the Dune books](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mentat).
