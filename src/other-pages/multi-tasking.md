# Multi Tasking

Research generally suggests that multitasking - trying to do two jobs at once - is less productive than focusing on one job and then another. The brain is not very good at multitasking.

See [this article](http://www.nytimes.com/2007/03/25/business/25multi.html?_r=1) in the New York Times.
