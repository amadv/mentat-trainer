# Improve Your Surroundings

Maybe it's time to get out of the city. [This article](http://www.boston.com/bostonglobe/ideas/articles/2009/01/04/how_the_city_hurts_your_brain/) from the Boston Globe suggests that city life can negatively affect mental performance and mood in a number of ways. Even something as simple as whether or not you can see greenery from your window appears to have an effect.
