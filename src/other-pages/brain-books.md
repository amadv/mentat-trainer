# Brain Books

This page is to keep track of books relevant to using your brain and becoming a better thinker.

Relevant books
--------------

* [MindPerformanceHacks](/books/mind-performance-hacks) - linked with the Mentat Wiki
* [The overflowing brain - Information overload and the limits of working memory](http://www.amazon.com/Overflowing-Brain-Information-Overload-Working/dp/0195372883) - by Torkel Klingberg
* The Owner's Manual for the Brain - by Pierce J Howard. Describes itself as giving "Everyday applications from mind-brain research. Very long (about 1000 pages), with a lot of useful information.
* [Pragmatic Thinking and Learning - Refactor Your Wetware](http://pragprog.com/titles/ahptl/pragmatic-thinking-and-learning) by Andy Hunt
* [Rules for the Direction of the Mind](http://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Rules_for_the_Direction_of_the_Mind) by [René Descartes](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_Descartes)
* Has anyone here read: John Medina "Brain Rules" 2010 ? (I first heard of it via ["Brain Rules: Why a Daily Standup Should Be in the Afternoon"](http://matthewrupert.net/2011/06/26/brain-rules-why-a-daily-standup-should-be-in-the-afternoon/) )
