# Brain Storming

Brain Storming
==============

Thought precedes action. When we want to create a list of our thoughts or ideas in order to record and keep track of them, we brainstorm. In a group setting, BrainStorming is a way of organizing a collective thought process.

Tools for Brain Storming
------------------------

Tools for brainstorming include systems of [NoteTaking](/other-pages/note-taking), software that supports brainstorming, and processes for organizing CollectiveCreativity.

* A [MindMap](/other-pages/mind-mapping) is a way of collecting ideas that is very appropriate to the non-linear nature of ideation. FreeMind is a free, open-source application for creating your own [MindMap](/other-pages/mind-mapping)s, available at the [FreeMind Wiki](http://freemind.sourceforge.net/wiki/index.php/Main_Page). There are also tutorials on [how to do a mind map](http://www.jcu.edu.au/studying/services/studyskills/mindmap/howto.html) and a collection of [mind mapping resources](http://members.optusnet.com.au/~charles57/Creative/Mindmap/index.html). [BrainStorming software](http://homepage.mac.com/penagoscorzo/creativity/brainstorming-software/) Description about brainstorming software.
* Lion Kimbro  wrote a book called "How to Make a Complete Map of Every Thought you Think", available [in its entirety](http://www.speakeasy.org/~lion/nb/).
