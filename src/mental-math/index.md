Mental Math Sites
=================

One of the best places to learn about the art and science of mental math is the [MentalCalculation mailing list](http://groups.yahoo.com/group/MentalCalculation/).

There's an excellent collection of over 400 tips and tricks at [BEATCALC: Beat the Calculator](http://mathforum.org/k12/mathtips/beatcalc.html).

* [Speed Arithmetic](http://www.asiteaboutnothing.net/p_speed-arithmetic.html) A collection of mental math techniques found in various books
* [Memory and Mental Calculation World Records](http://recordholders.org/en/list/memory.html)
* [Mental Calculation World Cup](http://recordholders.org/en/events/worldcup/)
* ["What's All This Mental Math Stuff, Anyhow?"](http://elecdesign.com/Articles/ArticleID/12165/12165.html) article by Bob Pease 2006-03-30
* [Wikipedia:Mental\_calculation](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_calculation)
